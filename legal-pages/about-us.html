PX League is an Arabic sports platform dedicated to football. We bring together everything a fan needs every day: match schedules, live scores, the events and line-ups of every match, league standings, Arab and world football news, and a free predictions competition.

Our mission

We want following football in Arabic to be fast, clear and reliable. That is why www.px-league.site is built mobile-first, light and quick, with an interface that puts the information right in front of you.

What we offer

  • Today's matches and live scores: kick-off times in Cairo time, with the score and minute updating automatically during play.
  • A page for every match: goals, cards and substitutions, line-ups and formations, stadium, referee and broadcaster, plus penalty shootouts and two-leg aggregates when they apply.
  • League standings: the major European leagues, Arab leagues and continental competitions.
  • News and analysis: coverage of the biggest football stories, written by our editorial team.
  • Predictions competition: predict results, earn points and compete on the leaderboard, free and with no betting.
  • The PX League app: available on Google Play and the App Store.

Our editorial standards

  • Accuracy before speed: we verify a story with a reliable source before publishing it, and credit the source when we quote it.
  • News and opinion kept apart: we clearly distinguish news from opinion and analysis.
  • Open corrections: when we find a mistake, we correct it in the article itself without delay.
  • Impartiality: we cover every club and national team by the same standards.

Data sources

Match, score and standings data comes from specialist sports data providers and is updated continuously. For the limits of its accuracy, see our Disclaimer.
