The PX League team is happy to hear from you, whether it is a question, a suggestion, a correction to a score or story, or a request about advertising or content rights. We usually reply within 48 hours.

For a faster reply, use one of these subject lines

  • "Correction": with the page link, the match or story, and the correct information and its source.
  • "Advertising": with your organisation's name and a short description of the proposed collaboration.
  • "Takedown request - DMCA": with the details listed in the copyright section of our Terms of use.
  • "Account data": to request a copy of your predictions-account data or its deletion; please send it from the email address your account is registered with.

We handle your messages in line with our Privacy policy and use your email address only to reply to you.
